A one-page newspaper front page drawn entirely in code: 27 stories from
Thursday 26 November 2026, each verified as having happened or first been
reported that day.

**The front page**

- **Lead 1 — New York:** Macy's 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed to
  Saturday 28 November, 8:30am ET — the first weather postponement in its
  100 years, forced by a nor'easter with gusts to 60 mph. NBC re-airs the
  2025 parade on Thursday instead.
- **Lead 2 — Geneva:** World Chess Championship Game 3, level 1–1 after two
  draws, Sindarov with White from 3pm CET.
- **Lead 3 — Mumbai:** 18 years since the 26/11 attacks.
- **Five more:** the EU special summit, India Constitution Day (77 years),
  IITs adding 6,000 tech seats, the India–Nepal ₹15,000-crore package, and
  India's space economy growing from $9bn to $45bn.
- Plus a dot map with numbered pins, 19 one-line stories, a day-in-numbers
  strip, a UTC timeline of the day, and a date-check box naming the big
  stories left out because they are *not* 26 November (Shenzhou-24, Samara
  refineries, Nvidia −7%, the US tariff plan, and more from 23–25 Nov).

Region tally: EUROPE 3 · AMERICAS 5 · MIDDLE EAST 2 · ASIA 16 · OCEANIA 1.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # renders entry.png (1024×1024)
python3 main.py out.png    # renders to a path of your choice
```

Stdlib + Pillow only. The render is deterministic — two runs produce
byte-identical PNGs.
